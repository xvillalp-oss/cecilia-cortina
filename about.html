Hello, I'm Cecilia.

My story

Since I was little, I knew I wanted to create something that felt meaningful.

I photographed my first wedding almost by accident, and everything clicked. I fell in love with the privilege of documenting real moments — quiet, joyful, emotional, and completely unrepeatable.

Knowing that these photographs will become part of a family's history is what inspires me every time I pick up my camera.

How I work

I don't approach weddings as something to control, but something to witness. Every couple is different, and every story carries its own rhythm. I take the time to understand yours, so what we create feels true and lasting.

I also understand that being photographed can feel unfamiliar. That's why I approach each wedding with sensitivity and respect — creating an environment where you can feel at ease, without pressure to perform or be anything other than yourselves.

Years from now, these images will become part of your memory, your family, your history. I don't take that lightly.
